# Icon credits

The rendered upgrade icons in `rendered/` are made for the game: rendered in Blender from
`tools/research_icons/`, the Speed icon from the game's own bus model.

The upgrade line icons, the trees' (rocket, globe, heart), and the research screen's lock are from [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) (`lucide-static` 0.460.0), as the resource bar's are (`game/solar_system/icons/`), with the stroke set to white so a `modulate` tints them.
